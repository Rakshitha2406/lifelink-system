EMERGENCY HEALTHCARE DECISION SUPPORT

LifeLink — Smart Emergency Blood Availability & Matching System

A centralized digital platform connecting emergency hospitals, accredited blood banks, and verified voluntary donors to eliminate life-threatening delays in blood availability.

Abstract & Platform Overview

This project aims to develop a smart and centralized platform that helps patients and hospitals quickly find suitable blood sources during emergency situations. The system considers important factors such as blood group, required blood units, hospital location, urgency level, and blood availability to identify nearby compatible sources. It uses location-based and priority-based matching to connect emergency blood requirements with suitable blood banks, hospitals, or registered sources. The platform also helps reduce the time and effort involved in manual blood searching and communication. Overall, the proposed system aims to provide a faster, organized, and reliable approach to emergency blood matching, thereby supporting better emergency response and community healthcare services.

Problem Statement

Context: During medical emergencies, finding the required blood group within a short time can be difficult. Hospitals and patients often depend on manual searches, phone calls, and personal contacts.

1. Difficulty in Finding Blood Finding rare or matched blood groups quickly during trauma emergencies is severely challenging.
2. Scattered Information Availability data is fragmented across isolated blood banks without unified digital visibility.
3. Time Delay (Golden Hour) Manual phone calling wastes precious minutes while patients require immediate transfusion.

Platform Objectives

  • Quick Blood Matching: Automatically identify clinically compatible sources based on component rules.
  • Location-Based Search: Compute Haversine distances to recommend nearest centers.
  • Urgency-Based Prioritization: Dynamically weigh distance and stock during Critical Code Red scenarios.
  • Multi-Source Shortage Pooling: Split large blood requisitions across multiple facilities when one center has insufficient units.
  • Reduce Search Time: Slash emergency search delays from hours to seconds.

Scope of Features

  • Blood Requirement Registration: Collect blood group, component, units, location, urgency.
  • Directory & Availability: Real-time inventory tracking across blood banks & hospital storage units.
  • Decision-Support Algorithm: Multi-factor scoring (Distance 35%, Stock 35%, Affinity 15%, Verification 15%).
  • Workflow Lifecycle: Submitted → Matched → Confirmed → Fulfilled.
  • Voluntary Donors Network: Direct citizen registration and proximity emergency notifications.

System Architecture & Technology Stack

Layer Technology Used Role & Responsibilities
Frontend UI HTML5, CSS3 Modern Theme, Vanilla JS, Leaflet.js Responsive emergency requisition interface, dynamic stock editing, live maps, and notification drawer.
Backend Application Python 3 (Flask RESTful API) Routing, data validation, smart matching algorithm execution, CORS support, and authentication.
Database Layer MySQL Database (with PyMySQL & SQLAlchemy) Relational storage for facilities, inventory levels, blood requests, donor registries, and audit logs.
Matching Algorithm Haversine Distance & Knapsack Pooling Engine Calculates great-circle distances, evaluates clinical compatibility, and formulates multi-source pooling plans.
Expected Healthcare Outcomes
• Faster Blood Identification: Reduce search time during critical emergencies.
• Improved Emergency Response: Help hospitals respond rapidly to trauma calls.
• Better Accessibility: Centralize scattered blood availability information.
• Community Support: Connect hospitals, blood banks, and voluntary donors seamlessly.
